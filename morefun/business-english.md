# 商务英语学习笔记

## 第 1 课：Clarifying & Asking for Details

### 本课笔记

| 用途 | 句型 |
|---|---|
| 没听清 | **Sorry, I didn’t catch that. Could you say that again?** |
| 没理解具体意思 | **Could you clarify what you mean by X?** |
| 针对某个模糊词追问 | **Just to clarify, when you say X, do you mean A, B, or C?** |
| 问具体标准 | **What level of X would be acceptable?** |
| 问目标 | **What X target are you aiming for?** |
| 问时间计划 | **Do you have a specific timeline in mind?** |
| 问完成日期 | **Do you have a target date for X?** |
| 问对方最关注什么 | **Which X are you most concerned about?** |
| 请对方重新讲某部分 | **Could you walk me through that part again?** |
| 确认具体定义 | **Are you mainly referring to A, B, or both?** |

### 优先记住

> **Could you clarify what you mean by X?**

> **Just to clarify, when you say X, do you mean A, B, or C?**

> **Do you have a specific X in mind?**

这三个模板可以套用在大多数客户需求场景。

### 例句

> Do you have a specific **timeline** in mind?

> Do you have a specific **availability target** in mind?

> Do you have a specific **latency requirement** in mind?

## 第 2 课：Confirming Understanding

### 本课笔记

| 用途 | 句型 |
|---|---|
| 确认整体理解 | **Just to make sure I understand correctly, ...** |
| 简洁确认 | **So, if I understand correctly, ...** |
| 复述对方意思 | **So, what you’re saying is that ...** |
| 确认行动项 | **Just to confirm, we’re going to ...** |
| 表达 A 比 B 优先 | **We’re prioritizing A over B.** |
| 表达 A 优先级更高 | **A is a higher priority than B.** |
| 表达“不是硬性要求” | **X is not a strict requirement.** |
| 表达分阶段执行 | **We’ll do X in phases.** |
| 表达逐步进行 | **We’ll do X gradually.** |

### 优先记住

> **So, if I understand correctly, ...**

> **Just to confirm, ...**

> **X is not a strict requirement.**

### 例句

> So, if I understand correctly, you want to minimize downtime, but you don’t require zero downtime, right?

> So, if I understand correctly, minimizing downtime is important, but zero downtime is not a strict requirement, right?

## 第 3 课：Expressing Opinions & Concerns

### 本课笔记

| 用途 | 句型 |
|---|---|
| 表达观点 | **From my perspective, ...** |
| 表达担忧 | **One concern I have is that ...** |
| 提醒一个考虑点 | **One thing to consider is ...** |
| 承认优点后转折 | **I understand that X, but ...** |
| 表达权衡 | **X may reduce A, but it could increase B.** |
| 表达长期判断 | **In the long run, ...** |
| 表达更好的选项 | **I think X is the better option because ...** |
| 表达运维投入 | **require more operational effort** |
| 表达运维复杂度 | **reduce operational complexity** |
| 表达维护风险 | **increase maintenance risk** |

### 优先记住

> **From my perspective, ...**

> **One concern I have is that ...**

> **One thing to consider is ...**

> **I understand that X, but ...**

### 例句

> My concern is that even if the performance is good, the team is not familiar with the technology, which could create maintenance risks after implementation.

> One concern I have is that although the performance may be good, the team is not familiar with the technology, which could increase maintenance risk after implementation.

### 用法提醒

- `even if X, Y` 和 `although X, Y` 后面不再加 `but`。
- 使用 `maintenance risk`、`operational risk` 或 `increase maintenance risk`，不使用 `risk of the maintenance`。
- 指代新技术时，用 `the technology` 比 `it` 更清楚。

## 第 4 课：Agreeing & Disagreeing Professionally

### 本课笔记

| 用途 | 句型 |
|---|---|
| 直接同意 | **I agree with that.** |
| 表示认可 | **That makes sense to me.** |
| 部分同意 | **I agree with the overall direction, but...** |
| 先认可再提担忧 | **I see your point, but one concern I have is that...** |
| 温和不同意 | **I’m not sure I fully agree with that.** |
| 提供另一个角度 | **Another way to look at it is...** |
| 承认优点再转折 | **I agree that X, but I’m not sure Y.** |
| 表达长期风险 | **In the long run, ...** |
| 表达“现在忽略，之后代价更高” | **Ignoring X now could lead to higher Y later.** |
| 表达某方案可能导致风险 | **X could lead to higher costs / more complexity / more risk.** |

### 优先记住

> **I see your point, but...**

> **I agree with the overall direction, but...**

> **I agree that X, but I’m not sure Y.**

> **Another way to look at it is...**

### 实用搭配

| 表达 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| **in the long run** | 长期来看 |
| **upfront cost** | 前期成本 |
| **operational complexity** | 运维复杂度 |
| **maintenance cost** | 维护成本 |
| **lead to higher costs** | 导致更高成本 |
